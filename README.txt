PHOTOS GO HERE

Save a photo with one of the names below and redeploy. It appears on the
site automatically. Use .jpg, .jpeg, .png, .webp or .avif.

  hero.jpg         One whole pizza from directly above, centered. Square.
                   At least 1600 x 1600. (Home page, top.)
  interior.jpg     The dining room. Upright, 4:5. At least 1200 x 1500.
  atmosphere.jpg   A detail: the oven, a table, hands at work. Upright, 4:5.
                   At least 1000 x 1250.

Menu item photos go in the menu folder, named after the item's id from
src/content/menu.ts, for example:

  menu/butter-chicken-pizza.jpg
  menu/chicken-curry-wrap.jpg

Landscape 4:3 or square both work. At least 1600 px on the long side for
the four dishes featured on the home page, 800 px for the rest.

Full instructions are in the README at the top of the project.
